How this flow maps to the app

  1. Site: the site list, Coordinates, a click on the Moon (as today). Screen an area is today's region + Where to land, offered as help, not a step.
  2. Date: today's time-bar popover (landing, stay) plus the landing-day strip, moved into the panel with a 4-week calendar.
  3. Verdict: one place for what the challenge asks: Sun elevation, time to sunset / Earthset, sunlit and Earth hours, longest dark against the battery, power and comms graded (today split between the site card, Details and Power / Comms).
  4. Compare: today's Details → Compare and the slip, as a table you add columns to (slip, other site, best date nearby); any column can become the plan.
  5. Go deeper: the rover route, Finalise and Execute, unchanged, after the four steps.

The steps are a guide, not a gate: every step stays clickable, and the Moon, the dial and the time bar are always there.
